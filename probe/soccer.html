We have leagues for players of all competitive levels, so sign up with Mid State Sports Leagues today!

Soccer League Details

  • 8-game guarantee (including playoffs for all teams)
  • Time: 2, 25-minute halves
  • Jerseys not required, same color shirt only
  • League Cost: See the tables below for Team and Individual cost per league
  • Indoor Soccer Facility: Boost Fit Club (Nashville’s nicest turf facility)
  • Midweek Game Times: Between 6-10pm
  • Sunday Game Times: Between 12-6pm outdoor & 8am-10pm indoor
  • Outdoor Soccer Style: 6v6 (2 females min in co-ed leagues)
  • Indoor Soccer/Futsal Style: 5v5 or 7v7 (2 females min in co-ed leagues)
  • Ages 18+
  • About 35+ Indoor League: Teams may have 2 players that are ages 30-34, remainder of team must be 35+
  • Note on Divisions: For leagues with divisions labeled “Rec, Competitive, A/B, etc.,” teams may specify which division they prefer to be placed in. However, MSSL reserves the right to move teams to a different division within the same league when deemed necessary.
  • For player eligibility rules and roster rules please see our FAQ’s page.
  • Weather Updates: All updates to Soccer leagues related to weather will be posted to our Facebook and X (Twitter) accounts. Be sure to follow those accounts for updates related to rainouts, weather changes, or if a league is playing tonight or not!
  • Forfeits: If you know your team will forfeit a game, please send a courtesy e-mail to us on our CONTACT PAGE.

Payment Information

We offer three options for registration payments:

  1. Team Cost: Team Captain registers and pays for their team’s entry into a league.
  2. Individual Cost: Register and pay as a Free Agent Individual and we will place you on a team.
  3. Split Payments with Teammates: When registering a team, choose to split payments! The team fee will be divided by the number of players on your roster. Players will receive an invite to join and play, and the captain can track payments in our Player Dashboard.

We have NO HIDDEN FEES in our league registration system. If a team registration fee is $500, it will be the same amount regardless of the number of players on your roster. We DO NOT charge fees to add players to your roster!
